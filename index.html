About Me

Bridging AI and Biomedicine

I am an Assistant Professor at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai in New York City, specializing in Medicine, Hematology, and Medical Oncology. My work stands at the forefront of translational research, applying cutting-edge artificial intelligence to solve complex biological problems.

My research focuses on dissecting the mechanisms of cancer biology, hematopoiesis, and immunology using single-cell multiomics and machine learning. By developing evidence-based AI systems, I aim to bridge the gap between computational discovery and clinical application, creating tools that are not only statistically rigorous but also interpretable for clinicians.

As the architect of the Universal Skill Definition Language (USDL), I am pioneering a standardized framework that allows biomedical AI agents to operate across platforms—scaling scientific analysis from the bench to the bedside.
